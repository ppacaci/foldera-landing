The rules (read this once)

Foldera is a commitment-based structure. You pick a fixed term, you complete it, you receive the outcome. If you break the commitment, principal costs apply. The goal is long-term stability — not short-term cycling.

1) Non-compounding
Rewards do not auto-roll. Finishing a term does not automatically compound into a new term. Renewals (if you choose) follow the terms of the new period.
Why: prevents hidden compounding assumptions and keeps outcomes auditably simple.
2) 12-month repricing
The 12-month term is repriced each year. If you do 12 months and then renew, the new year may have different ranges.
Why: keeps the system sustainable across changing conditions.
3) Long-term lock is different
The 24-month term locks conditions upfront (within the stated ranges). Long-term is rewarded with better efficiency.
Why: fewer interruptions and lower liquidity pressure over time.
4) Early exits are intentionally expensive
Early exit forfeits rewards/bonuses and applies a principal cost. This protects long-term participants and the structure.
You can still reconsider within 48 hours for a flat 2% cost.

FAQ

Short answers. Clear rules. No marketing smoke.

Is Foldera live today? Can I deposit?
No. Foldera is currently in early access. This page documents the rules and proposed structure. We are not accepting funds on this website today.
What are the exact return ranges?
Core (no bonus):
• 6 months: +4% to +4.5% (total)
• 12 months: +12% to +16% (annual target)
• 24 months: +26% to +35% (total)
Bonus tiers (extended terms) are listed in the Bonus section and require full completion.
Can I exit early and keep rewards (or some rewards)?
No. Rewards and bonuses require full term completion. Any early exit forfeits rewards/bonuses. Early exit may also apply a principal cost, as shown in the Exit Rules section.
What is the 48-hour reconsideration window?
You can exit within 48 hours of entry with a flat 2% principal cost. No rewards. No bonuses. It exists to reduce hesitation while preventing free cycling.
What are checkpoint exits?
Checkpoint exits are a limited early-access feature for the first 30 participants. If you exit exactly at a checkpoint, your principal cost is 0%, but all rewards and bonuses are forfeited. Checkpoints: 12-month term at month 6; 24-month term at months 9 and 15.
What does “non-compounding” mean here?
Rewards do not automatically roll into a new term. Finishing a term does not auto-create a new position that compounds. If you renew, you are entering a new term under the current pricing for that time period.
Is this an investment offer or financial advice?
No. This website is informational and for early-access interest validation. Nothing here is a solicitation to accept funds, and nothing is financial advice.
Who is Foldera for?
Long-term ETH holders who prefer clarity: defined terms, defined exits, and defined bonus conditions — not hype, not vague APY promises.
Will these rules change later?
If the product goes live, the terms shown here are intended to be the ones users agree to at entry. The 12-month term is explicitly repriced each year; 24-month terms lock conditions upfront within the stated ranges.
